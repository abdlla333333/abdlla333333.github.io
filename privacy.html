The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your card or payment details. Google Play shares only purchase confirmations with the game so the item can be delivered.
